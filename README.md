# 🛒 Zepto Inventory & Pricing Analysis (SQL)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce inventory data from Zepto using SQL to generate business insights related to pricing, discounts, stock availability, and product categories.

The goal is to simulate how data analysts work with real-world retail datasets to support business decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives
- Analyze product pricing and discount patterns  
- Identify high-value and low-performing products  
- Evaluate stock availability and inventory distribution  
- Generate category-level revenue insights  

---

## Tools Used
- SQL (PostgreSQL)

---

## Key Analysis Performed

### Data Exploration
- Counted total records
- Checked null values
- Identified distinct product categories
- Analyzed stock availability

### Data Cleaning
- Removed products with invalid pricing
- Converted prices from paise to rupees

### Business Insights
- Top products based on discount percentage  
- High-value products that are out of stock  
- Estimated revenue by category  
- Price per gram analysis  
- Inventory weight distribution  

---

## Key Insights
- Identified categories with highest revenue potential  
- Found pricing inconsistencies in product listings  
- Highlighted out-of-stock premium products  
- Evaluated discount strategies across categories  

---

## Files
- `queries.sql` → All SQL queries used in analysis  

---

## Outcome
This project demonstrates SQL skills for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and generating business insights from real-world e-commerce data.
